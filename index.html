Street Foods


Ice cream

Ice cream is a cold, sweet, and creamy frozen dessert made from milk and sugar that comes in many delicious flavors.


French fries

French fries are deep-fried, salted strips of potato that are crispy on the outside and soft on the inside.


Sandwich

A sandwich is a food item consisting of vegetables, sliced cheese, or meat placed between slices of bread, or more generally, any dish where two or more pieces of bread serve as a container or wrapper for another food type.


Burger

A burger is a popular sandwich consisting of a cooked patty—usually made of ground beef—placed inside a sliced bun and often topped with cheese, vegetables, and condiments.
